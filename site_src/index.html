The problem

Money leaves quietly, one short payment at a time

A brand ships product and invoices a retailer. The retailer pays, minus claims. Each claim can be accepted, written off, or disputed and sometimes won back. Few finance teams can see the whole picture.

Many reasons, little visibility

Shortages, promotion claims, compliance fines, pricing differences and damage all arrive as one short payment.

Time works against you

Old deductions are rarely won back, and dispute windows close. Most of the open balance is already months old.

Numbers that do not add up

One invoice has many deductions and each can have a dispute, so careless joins double-count dollars and mislead.

Limits

What this does not claim

  • The data are synthetic. The findings demonstrate a method; they say nothing about real retailers.
  • Dispute-selection bias is simulated, not measured. The recoverable range depends on assumptions.
  • The forecast shows no winner between the two models on {{ forecast.origins }} overlapping origins.
  • Returns, credit memos, chargebacks and partial-payment balances are not modelled.
  • Win rate and days to resolve cover resolved disputes only, so they lean toward fast outcomes.
  • This is a full-rebuild prototype: no incremental loads, access control or monitoring.
